Workforce Engagement, Actions & Visibility Environment

Every part of your workforce, woven together.

WEAVE is the planned web dashboard that grows out of the Coalesce Excel add-in. Hiring, travel, training, awards and the rest of the workforce business, in one place, with new panels added release by release.

The 3D hero is being built now. Here's the idea.

  • A quilt is a dashboard. Each block is one panel, and the strips sewn between the blocks become the gaps between the tiles.
  • Click the quilt and it unstitches into a dashboard you can rearrange. Click again and it sews itself back together, in your order.
  • The quilt grows with the product. Live panels are sewn in and planned ones are pinned in place. On release morning, the new blocks get sewn in while you watch.
  • Coalesce lives on as the Manpower patch.
